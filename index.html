Aviation produces roughly 2% of the world's CO2 today, a share projected to climb to 12-27% by 2050. Around 40% of those emissions come from short-haul routes, which is exactly where electric aircraft fit. They fly with no operational emissions and need the lightest infrastructure of any regional transport mode.

Cost-Effective

75% cost savings
in fuel and 50%
in maintenance

Going electric rewrites the economics of regional flying. Electric aircraft cost less to buy, run and service. Simple, dependable electric motors cut maintenance costs by about 90% versus turboprops, smart electronic monitoring means fewer inspections, and fuel costs fall by 50-75%.
